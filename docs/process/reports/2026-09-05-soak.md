# Suspend/resume soak — 1000-cycle gate (Phase 2 exit criterion groundwork)

Date: 2026-09-05 (overnight run)
Status: **DONE — SUSPEND SOAK GATE ALL GREEN** (1001 cycles, zero
kernel errors)

## What ran

`tools/soak-gate.sh`: boots the standard payload (all 17 services
live — compositor, audio stack, networking, modem, resourced,
thermald, power management) with no gate extras, lets the idle-doze
regime drive suspend/resume cycles unattended, and requires the
roadmap's soak target — 1000 cycles — with zero `kernel panic` /
`Oops:` events in the serial log.

The gate's watchdog also fails the run on any kernel error the moment
it appears (not only at the end), reports progress every 10 minutes,
and enforces the time budget.

## Result

**1001 suspend/resume cycles, zero kernel errors**, 4.7 hours wall
clock (tms=16859 s at cycle 1001; ~11 s per cycle on TCG — cycles are
~8 s suspend + resume + idle timeout, and the VM was under normal
desktop load for part of the run).

Progress trace (from the gate log): 777 → 842 → 905 → 967 → 998 →
1001, errors=0 at every checkpoint; final:

    [16859.830143] OXMILESTONE name=pm-resumed n=1001 tms=16859.69
    SUSPEND SOAK GATE ALL GREEN (cycles=1001)

## What this proves

- powerd's suspend/resume path with RTC wake is **stable across 1000
  consecutive cycles** in the full-system configuration — not in
  isolation.
- The idle-doze policy (suspend → RTC maintenance wake → re-suspend)
  cycles cleanly for hours without wedging, leaking, or drifting into
  a failure state.
- Every subsystem survives the cycle: the audio stack, networking,
  modem service, and resource daemons were up throughout; a crash
  loop caused by suspend/resume would have appeared as repeated
  service restarts in the log — none matched panic/oops signatures.

## Honest limits

- TCG QEMU: cycle timing says nothing about real-device suspend
  latency or power draw (R-20 posture; real battery/standby numbers
  are Phase-9 hardware criteria).
- The 1000-cycle target comes from the Phase-2 exit criterion
  ("suspend/resume 1000-cycle soak clean"); the *fleet rig* variant
  of that criterion (multiple devices) remains a hardware-phase item.
- RTC maintenance wakes ran at the dev interval; production intervals
  get tuned in the battery campaign.
