# Gesture navigation — chunk 30, 2026-09-08

## Claim

The compositor now distinguishes TAPS from SWIPES, and the shell maps
edge-aware swipe gestures to navigation:
- **swipe up from the bottom third** → go home (closes the switcher or
  activity panel)
- **swipe down from the top strip** → toggle the activity panel
Anything mid-screen or lateral is ignored — no hidden actions away
from the edges. The unlock pad also proves the underlying change: all
clicks in every gate now flow through the new delivery path.

## Design decisions

- **Clicks moved to release.** Clicks used to fire at PRESS time, so a
  swipe would also have delivered a phantom click to whatever it
  started on. Now a press is recorded (with its target window), and on
  release it is classified: displacement under 60 px on both axes = a
  tap (click at the press point, window-relative); a longer press = a
  swipe named by its dominant-axis direction of travel. Focus/raise
  still happen at press — only delivery changed. Every gate's pad taps
  exercise this path on every run.
- **EventHub<T>.** The per-window queue + condvar hub is now generic:
  `ClickHub = EventHub<ClickEv>` and `GestureHub = EventHub<GestureEv>`
  share one implementation. The IPC gains a `gesture-recv` op (same
  shape and lock-free routing as `click-recv` — input waits never hold
  the compositor lock).
- **Edge-aware mapping is a pure function** (`map_gesture`): the
  bottom third is the home-strip, the top bar is the alerts strip,
  everything else is inert. Tested exhaustively at the boundaries.
- The shell drains queued gestures with a non-blocking poll each loop
  iteration — gestures are rare, the queue absorbs them, and no extra
  blocking wait is added to the main loop.

## Gate evidence (`tools/gesture-gate.sh` ALL GREEN)

A `qmp_swipe` helper injects down → stepped absolute moves → up:
- swipe down (640,40)→(640,300): `shell-alerts-open` — the activity
  panel opens from the top edge.
- swipe up (640,650)→(640,350): `shell-gesture-home` — back home from
  the panel.
- switcher open (top-left trigger), then swipe up from the bottom:
  `shell-gesture-home` closes the switcher too.
- **negative leg**: a mid-screen swipe down (300→520) does NOT open
  the panel (open-count unchanged over 8 s) — edges only.
- The PIN unlock before all of it proves click-on-release: the pad is
  hit-tested on the same path.

## Unit tests (workspace gate)

- oxcom `gestures_need_travel_and_name_their_direction`: tap under the
  threshold, up/down/left/right by sign, dominant-axis choice on
  diagonals, exactly-at-threshold counts as a swipe.
- oxcom `gesture_hub_queues_per_window`: per-window queueing, drain,
  drop-window isolation.
- ox-shell `gestures_map_by_edge_and_direction`: the edge boundaries
  (bottom-third, top-bar strip) and every ignored combination.

## Instructive findings

1. **The two test call sites** of `handle_input_wait` (the session and
   starvation tests) needed the gesture hub too — the workspace gate's
   test build caught both before the payload could ship.
2. `EventHub<T>` needed a MANUAL `Default` impl: the derive would add
   a `T: Default` bound no event type satisfies.
3. The apply-loop closure had to become `mut` to own the press state.
