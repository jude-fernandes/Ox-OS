## What the exit sweep caught (the sweep doing its job)

The full-sweep walk of 2026-10-05 17:53 UTC returned three reds. All
three are migration regressions from chunks 68–70, each root-caused
from the boot's own serial log before any fix was written:

1. **modem-gate — permstored dead at boot.** The gate's payload clone
   carried the LUKS-formatted data.img; this gate never unlocks it, so
   /data stayed read-only and permstored's chunk-70 honest-FATAL
   ("a grant that cannot be saved must not look saved") killed it —
   rc=1 in the log. Before chunk 70 the same boot silently served
   unsaved in-memory grants: the honest failure replaced a false pass.
   Fix: modem-gate-run.sh now forces the fresh plain-ext4 data volume
   (the bt/gnss runner convention every other gate already followed),
   and modem-selftest waits for permstored.sock before seeding its
   grant.
2. **gnss-gate — grant keyed to the payload field, not the caller.**
   The selftest granted `gnssd` (the `fix {"app":...}` payload name)
   but the consult keys on the kernel comm of the caller — `oxctl`,
   which drives locationd. Fix: grant and deny-restore renamed to
   oxctl.
3. **messages-store-gate — the store writer starved by the consult
   layer.** messagesd polls oxmodemd's sms-list as the telephony
   writer; chunk 68's all-ops consult denied it (`modem-consult
   app=ox-messagesd mode=deny op=sms-list`, every poll, all boot) and
   nothing ever ingested. The poll is a SYSTEM path by design — the
   daemon writes the inbox, it is not a reader app. Fix: oxmodemd's
   sms-list serves the `ox-messagesd` comm as an identity-resolved
   system path (journaled `mode=system`); every other comm still
   consults, unknown identity still fails closed. A fleet-wide audit
   of daemon-to-daemon clients found no other edge of this class.
4. **messages-store-gate (second finding, from the solo re-run) — the
   chunk-41 walker race, resurrected by chunk 68.** With both ingest
   legs green the solo reached the deny/fake/grant matrix — and the
   fake leg read `deny` 1.2s after a consult served `fake`: two
   selftests (messages-store and fake-providers, plus gnss on
   location.read) walk modes through the SAME caller comm (oxctl),
   and post-68 the consult keys on the caller, so their flips stomp
   each other on the shared rows. The chunk-41 separation (distinct
   app rows per walker) is void — the payload app is display-only
   now. Fix: caller serialization — fake-providers-selftest waits for
   the messages-store and gnss walkers' conclusions before walking
   their rows (their restore-deny fires at their milestone; a 2s poll
   cannot overtake it). modem-selftest also gained its missing
   deny-restore. A solo-re-run finding: the formalized procedure
   paying for itself — the walk red alone would have hidden this one.
   The serialization's first version waited on the wrong channel
   (verdict milestones are console echoes; they never reach dmesg) —
   the re-solo caught it via the walker's entirely-absent DBG lines,
   and the selftests now journal kmsg completion markers that the
   waits grep.
